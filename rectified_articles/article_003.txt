## Maruti Suzuki’s Market Cap to Hit ₹5 Lakh Crore Mark Soon  

### Synopsis  
Maruti Suzuki, India’s largest automaker, is on the verge of joining the ₹5 lakh‑crore market‑capitalisation club. The company’s shares hit a record high today, rising nearly 1 % to a new peak of ₹15,384. So far in 2024, Maruti’s stock has gained an impressive 14 %, its biggest annual jump since 2012.  

## A Look at the Valuation Surge  

### Market Performance  
The latest surge in Maruti’s market value comes after a long wait of nearly 16 months. The company has added roughly ₹80,000 crore to its market capitalisation since crossing the ₹4 lakh‑crore mark in March 2024. Currently, only a handful of Indian companies have market capitalisations above ₹5 lakh crore, according to BSE data.  

## Share‑Price Surge Attributed to GST Rate Cuts  

### Government Impact  
The recent rally in Maruti’s shares can be largely attributed to the government’s decision to cut GST rates on automobiles. The move has raised hopes of a revival in passenger‑vehicle demand, especially for small cars that account for nearly half of Maruti’s revenue. Brokerage houses have also revised their target prices upward, viewing Maruti Suzuki as the biggest beneficiary of the GST reduction.  

## Small Cars Are the Biggest Losers  

### Market Shift  
Small cars—defined as petrol vehicles with engine capacity below 1,000 cc, diesel below 1,500 cc, and not exceeding four metres in length—have seen a decline in sales as buyers shift toward larger SUVs. However, with GST on these models reduced from 18 % to 12 %, expectations are high that demand for budget‑friendly cars will pick up again.  

## Brokerage Firms Raise Share‑Price Targets  

### Analyst Predictions  
Global brokerage firm Morgan Stanley has raised its share‑price target for Maruti Suzuki to ₹17,000 per share from ₹14,000. ICICI Securities has also increased its target to ₹17,000 per share and issued a “Buy” rating. Both firms believe that the recent GST cut across most auto segments could spur fresh demand momentum and improve affordability in the market.
