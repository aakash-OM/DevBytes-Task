## iPhone 17 Pro series gets revamped design, A18 Pro chip  

### Synopsis  
Apple unveiled its latest flagship smartphones at its September 2025 event: the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max. The new models feature a refreshed titanium design, a larger “full‑width” camera bar, and major camera upgrades. Both devices are powered by Apple’s new A18 Pro chip and ship with iOS 18 out of the box.

## Pricing and availability  

### Launch  
In India, the iPhone 17 Pro starts at **₹1,34,900** for the base 256 GB variant, while the iPhone 17 Pro Max begins at **₹1,49,900**. Both phones are offered in Cosmic Orange, Deep Blue, and Silver. Pre‑orders begin on **13 September 2025**, and the devices become available in stores on **20 September 2025**.

## Design and display  

### Design  
The iPhone 17 Pro series moves to a **grade‑5 titanium** frame, replacing the aluminum used on the previous generation. Apple also introduced a **graphene‑based heat spreader** that works alongside an upgraded vapor‑chamber system to keep performance throttling to a minimum during intensive tasks.  

- **iPhone 17 Pro**: 6.3‑inch Super Retina XDR display, 120 Hz ProMotion, up to 2,000 nits peak brightness.  
- **iPhone 17 Pro Max**: 6.7‑inch Super Retina XDR display, 120 Hz ProMotion, up to 2,200 nits peak brightness.  

Both screens support HDR 10, Dolby Vision, and an always‑on display mode.

## Major camera upgrades  

### Enhanced photography  
The Pro models now sport a **rectangular camera bar** that spans the full width of the back, giving the devices a distinctive look.  

- **Telephoto**: 48 MP sensor with 5× optical zoom (up to 12× digital zoom).  
- **Wide**: 48 MP main sensor with larger pixels for better low‑light performance.  
- **Ultra‑wide**: 12 MP sensor with a 120° field of view and improved autofocus.  

The front‑facing camera has been upgraded to a **12 MP sensor with autofocus**, supporting Night mode and 4K @ 60 fps video recording.

## A18 Pro chip and storage options  

### Tech specs  
The iPhone 17 Pro series is powered by Apple’s **A18 Pro** SoC, which Apple says delivers up to **40 % faster sustained CPU performance** and **2× higher GPU throughput** compared with the A17 Bionic. Key features include:  

- 6‑core CPU (2 performance cores, 4 efficiency cores)  
- 5‑core GPU with Apple‑custom graphics architecture  
- 16‑core Neural Engine capable of 35 TOPS (trillion operations per second)  
- Integrated 5 nm + + process with a graphene heat spreader for improved thermal management  

Storage starts at **256 GB** for both models. The iPhone 17 Pro is available with 256 GB, 512 GB, and 1 TB options, while the iPhone 17 Pro Max adds a **2 TB** variant for users who need massive on‑device capacity.
