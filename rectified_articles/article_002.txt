## Haryana Man Killed in Los Angeles After Confronting Public Urination  

### Synopsis  
A 28‑year‑old man from Barwala in Hisar district, Haryana, India, was shot dead in Los Angeles, California, on Saturday night after he confronted a person who was urinating on the roadside near his workplace. The victim has been identified as Kapil [surname not disclosed]. He was employed as a security guard at a retail store in Los Angeles.  

### Verbal Argument Escalated Into Fatal Shooting  
According to a statement from the Los Angeles Police Department (LAPD), Kapil approached a man he observed urinating in a public area and asked him to stop. A verbal dispute followed, after which the other individual allegedly produced a firearm and shot Kapil. He was rushed to a nearby hospital, where he was pronounced dead on arrival.  

### Family’s Grief  
Kapil’s relatives in India said they were informed of the incident by U.S. authorities. His uncle, Ramesh Kumar, who runs a tractor agency in Pillu Khera, Jind district, told *Hindustan Times*: “Our hopes for his future were shattered.” The family described the loss as a devastating blow. Kapil’s post‑mortem examination is scheduled for Wednesday, after which arrangements will be made for repatriation of his remains.  

### Repatriation Plans  
The family expects to incur a cost of roughly ₹15 lakh for transporting Kapil’s body back to India. Village sarpanch Suresh Gautam said the local administration would help the family coordinate with the Indian consulate and relevant U.S. agencies to facilitate the repatriation.  

### Background  
Media reports indicate that Kapil arrived in the United States in 2021. Details about the circumstances of his migration have not been independently verified, and no official source has confirmed the route or any payments made to agents.
