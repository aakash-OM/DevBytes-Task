## IMPPA asks PM Modi's help in lifting 'Bengal Files' ban
### Synopsis
The Indian Motion Picture Producers' Association (IMPPA) has written to Prime Minister Narendra Modi over the ongoing controversy surrounding The Bengal Files. The film was released on September 5 but is reportedly facing an blocked by most theaters in West Bengal. Despite being certified by the Central Board of Film Certification and legally permitted for public exhibition, the film is not being screened due to a collective decision by entertainment chains.

## Producers, distributors facing intimidation: IMPPA
### Film's plight
IMPPA's letter, addressed to Prime Minister Narendra Modi, said, "Though not officially banned, the film has been subjected to indirect restrictions that have denied the public its rightful opportunity to view it." The association claimed that theater owners were facing intimidation and were not receiving support from the state government. They also alleged that the state government, had failed to provide a safe environment for the film's release.
